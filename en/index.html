About the Institute

The Institute for Genealogy Research specializes in family history research, archival investigations, historical documentation, and professional family tree reconstruction.

We combine archival research, international databases, historical sources, and modern technologies to uncover and preserve family stories.

Our mission is to connect generations and preserve family heritage for future generations.

We provide professional services for individuals, families, researchers, institutions, and communities while maintaining the highest standards of accuracy, confidentiality, and source-based research.
