Collaborations, Research & Publications

This archive presents a selection of publications, research studies, books and projects in which Yehuda Aharon Horowitz has been involved over the years. Through his research activities, Yehuda Aharon Horowitz has contributed to numerous publications and scholarly projects in the fields of genealogy, Jewish history and heritage studies.
